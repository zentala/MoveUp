I'd love to send you a free assembled kit (no strings, no script).

I'm based in Poland, shipping anywhere in EU takes 2-4 days.

What do you think?

Best,
Pawel Zentala
zentala.io/desk

P.S. The app is built with Rust + Tauri (React frontend).
Full source on GitHub: [REPO URL]

### Cadence Rules

- Send max 4 outreach emails per day (avoid spam patterns)
- Space batches by category — don't hit all standing desk channels the same day
- Best send times: Tuesday-Thursday, 10:00-14:00 in creator's local timezone
- If a creator responds positively, prioritize shipping within 48 hours
- If a creator publishes a video, reshare within 2 hours on all channels
- Never send more than 2 follow-ups total per creator (initial + 1 follow-up)
